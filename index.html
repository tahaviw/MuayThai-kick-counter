AI Kick Counter is a smart training web app that uses artificial intelligence to detect and count your kicks in real time. Whether you're working on speed, power, or endurance, this app tracks your movement automatically — so you can focus 100% on your technique. Using advanced motion tracking, the system analyzes your body movement through your camera and accurately counts every clean kick. No manual input. No distractions. Just pure training.
